Hacking the Think Tank II · July 31, 2026

A 900-page bill gets a cost estimate and an op-ed. Whether it will actually work, nobody scores.

Implementation claims — enrollment, take-up, delivery — are argued before passage and forgotten after. A claim that can never be wrong is worth nothing to the people deciding.

What we built

Thesis: a bill goes in; accountable, scored forecasts come out.

Implementation analysis that can be wrong in public — countersignable goals, effects shown regardless, and every claim ending in a forecast scored against the government's own first prints.

Eval · davidgringras.github.io/elicitation-measurement

We measured the elicitation itself

  • Named bills make models ignore the text: we rewrote a $300/week provision to $100 and $900 — forecasts didn't move. Hide the name and they track the amounts; named, only structured elicitation fixes it, even at max reasoning effort
  • Best validated single-run setup — fable-5 · bill text · max effort: beats default prompting 26/36 and mechanical extrapolation 29/36 head-to-head across 8 bill types — and 17/17 where the statute actually moved the series
  • The same setup at default effort is one of the worst arms tested; pooled over repeats it's the best outright

Adversarial review caught the confound at 2pm; the preregistered rerun flipped the result by 3.

The closer

The first bill-conditional forecast cell

  • CRP enrolled acres — April's official first print: 26,182,019 vs the bill's 27,000,000 ceiling
  • P(enrollment path | this provision becomes law vs not) — condition anchored to the provision surviving into enacted text, checkable against govinfo
  • Resolver anchored to three FSA first prints we verified this afternoon — including the one-acre rounding quirk in FSA's own totals
  • Registered through the privileged custody path; scored against FSA's publications either way
The analysis didn't just critique the bill. It went on the record.
Path forward

Built to scale to millions of forecasts

  • One registration path for every forecast type — model, challenge, conditional
  • Case study = scoreboard: bill pages join live cells by series id
  • Identity is computed, not stored: template slugs + UUID permalinks; batched custody keeps verification O(days)
  • Eval-driven routing: cheap arms broadly, escalated effort where measurement says it pays
